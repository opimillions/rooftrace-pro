Privacy Policy

Last updated November 21, 2026. RoofTrace Pro is operated by First Call Public Adjusters in Southlake, Texas. This policy covers the RoofTrace Pro website and the RoofTrace Pro iPhone app.

What we collect

  • Account details: your name, email address, company details you choose to add, and a securely hashed password.
  • Properties and measurements: the addresses you look up, roof tracings, claim numbers and inspector names you enter, interior rooms you sketch or scan, and the reports you generate.
  • Room scans: when you scan a room with the iPhone app, the app uses the camera and LiDAR scanner on your phone to measure walls, doors, windows and fixtures. Only the measurements are sent to RoofTrace Pro. No photos or video of the room are uploaded.
  • Payments: handled by Stripe on our website. We never see or store your full card number.
  • Basic logs: such as sign-in times and IP addresses, used to keep accounts secure and the service running.

How we use it

To run your account, produce measurements, sketches and reports, send the emails you ask for (such as password resets), prevent abuse, and improve the service. We do not sell your personal information. The iPhone app does not track you across other companies' apps or websites.

Who we share it with

Only service providers that help us run RoofTrace Pro (for example hosting, map and satellite imagery providers, email delivery and Stripe for payments), and when required by law.

Your choices

  • Delete properties and rooms at any time.
  • Delete your account at any time under Account → Delete account. This removes your account, properties, rooms and reports and cancels any paid plan.
